02 — Programs

Three tracks, one connected curriculum.

Structured learning pathways from foundational concepts to hands-on, competition-ready skills. Tap any topic to see what it covers.

Robotics

  • The building blocks of a robot — structure, motors, power, and control — and how they work together to sense and act.

  • How robots perceive the world through sensors, and how actuators turn decisions into movement and action.

  • Hands-on coding with Arduino microcontrollers to control lights, motors, and sensors in real projects.

  • Combining robotics with artificial intelligence so machines can recognise, decide, and respond on their own.

  • How repetitive tasks are automated in the real world — from simple home devices to factory-scale systems.

  • Designing, building, and testing a robot end-to-end, then putting it to the test in competitive challenges.

Space & Aerospace

  • Exploring planets, stars, and the structure of our solar system as a foundation for space science.

  • The core physics of propulsion and thrust — how rockets escape gravity and reach orbit.

  • How satellites are built, launched, and used for communication, navigation, and observation.

  • Students design and build a working mini-satellite the size of a soft-drink can, then launch and test it.

  • Planning a full space mission — objectives, systems, and constraints — the way real space agencies do.

  • Projects modelled on real ISRO missions, connecting classroom learning to India's space achievements.

Quantum

  • The strange, foundational rules of quantum physics that govern matter at the smallest scales.

  • How quantum computers process information differently from classical computers, and why it matters.

  • How quantum principles enable new, highly secure ways of transmitting information.

  • Using quantum mechanics to build encryption that's fundamentally harder to break.

  • A look at emerging quantum applications — from sensing to materials science — on the near horizon.

  • Guided, student-led research projects that turn quantum concepts into original investigations.
